Google is building a Kubernetes for AI agents. It's called AX, Agent Executor.

Here's the problem. Most of us run agents on our laptops. For a business, that's a security risk. You can't see what they're doing, which jobs are running, or how much you're spending.

AX moves them onto shared infrastructure. Every agent runs isolated, in its own sandbox.

You write three things. A workspace, with your repos, MCP servers and skills. A model. And a task.

Then one command. AX sandboxes the agent and runs it.

You can watch it live, shell into the sandbox, or pause it and resume exactly where it left off.

And it's built for billions of tasks per cluster.

It's the same shift as software factories. Take agents off your machine, and treat them like any other infrastructure.
