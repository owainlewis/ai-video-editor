In four months, Anthropic went from about 2,500 changes a day to more than 30,000. No team can read all of that by hand.

So they stopped reviewing every change the same way. The change sets the risk, not the author. A classifier reads it and picks a tier.

Low risk. Docs, copy, additive changes behind a flag. No human needed once the AI review and security checks pass.

Medium risk. Ordinary product changes. One person reviews.

High risk. The riskiest code. Two people.

And whoever prompted the agent is on the hook for the change.

Some mistakes still get through, so everything is reversible. Flags turn off without a deploy.

Spend human attention where a mistake would hurt. Not on every line.
