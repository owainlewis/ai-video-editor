Google is building a Kubernetes for AI agents. It's called AX, Agent Executor.

Here's the problem. Agents aren't web services, and they aren't batch jobs. They hold state, they need isolation, and they can burn money in a loop if nobody's watching.

So AX makes them declarative. You write three things. A workspace, with your repos, MCP servers and skills. A model. And a task.

Then one command. AX sandboxes the agent and runs it.

You can watch it live, shell into the sandbox, or pause it and resume exactly where it left off.

It's built for billions of tasks per cluster. It's early, so expect breaking changes.
