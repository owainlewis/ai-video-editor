## Structure

- A graded talking head (teal shadows, warm skin, a dark room with lamps) carries most of it, with almost no hard cuts in the footage (two found). B-roll appears twice, briefly.
- Flat cards cut in on the big beats: cream on charcoal `#12191B`, deep green on white, cream on deep green `#234441`. That green and charcoal are very close to our ink and sage family.
- One line diagram (a diamond) drawn on a green card, with the captions moving inside it.

## The text kit: six treatments, reused

The same few treatments come back again and again, so the reel feels designed rather than decorated.

1. **Caption.** One or two words at a time, bold sans, lowercase, warm cream (not white), low centre on the chest. Size follows weight: connective words ("and so", "i've coined", "for them") are set at about 60% of normal size, so the eye skips them.
2. **Hit.** A number or key phrase in bold caps, larger, that **types on letter by letter** ("$1", "$10 M", "$10 MILLION") in about 0.5 s. Then a light serif italic tag types in under it, offset to the right ("net worth"). Bold sans and serif italic together: the same pairing as our Helvetica and Signifier.
3. **Title.** A concept's name in serif italic, two lines, the second indented right and tucked under the first ("diamond / promise"), typing on. It sits above a line diagram that draws on stroke by stroke as it types.
4. **Caption inside the diagram.** Once the diagram is drawn, the word-by-word caption moves into the shape's centre, so the picture and the words become one thing.
5. **List.** A bracketed caps header ("[OFFER]"), then items stack under it, centred, each landing on its word, all held until the list is complete.
6. **Card beat.** One huge caps word on a flat card, hard swapped on the beat ("35", then "YEARS"; "SH\*T", then "OFFER"). Rhythm comes from size contrast: "SAFE" huge, "for them" tiny, "to say" medium, "YES" huge.

## Sound

- A quiet bed under the voice.
- A censor bleep (about 1 kHz) on "SH\*T", then about 0.15 s of total silence before the next line: a pause used as punctuation.
- No other sound effects found.
