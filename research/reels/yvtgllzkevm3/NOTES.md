Sent by Owain on 2026-11-06: "one of the top Instagram influencers in this space... lots of really nice looking graphic elements". 36 s, 720x1280, -14.3 LUFS with a very tight range (LRA 1.8). A reel about a set of LinkedIn skills for Claude.

Read `sheet-2fps.jpg` first (a frame every 0.5 s, with times), then `look-a/b/c.jpg` (nine full frames), `motion-all.jpg` (accent word, marker, counter, call to action at 10 fps) and `spectrum-*.png` (sound).

## Structure

- Ten hard cuts in 36 s (`cuts.txt`), one every 3.3 s on average. Inside a long UI shot, something new lands every 0.4 to 0.6 s, so the frame never sits still.
- Three layouts: **split** (UI on top, speaker below, about 45%), **full-frame UI** on white or near black (about 35%) and **talking head** (about 20%).
- The speaker panel in a split has rounded top corners and a soft fade into the page above it, not a hard seam.

## Graphics

- **Rebuilt product UI, not screenshots.** LinkedIn's post composer, a comment thread, a profile card, a week planner, an "Algorithm check" panel and a Claude chat window, all redrawn large and crisp at about 2x real size. Note: the people and posts in them are made up (an "Alex Rivera"). We can take the craft but not that: our rule is never to invent UI, so we rebuild only what the real tool shows, with its real words.
- **Typing.** Text types into the composer and the chat with a blinking cursor. The next two or three letters show pale just before they land.
- **Rolling counter.** "Search appearances" rolls from 0,000 to 1,284 in about 0.8 s, with vertical motion blur on the digits. A small bar chart grows beside it, bar by bar, and a "+312%" tag pops in after it lands. Times in the planner roll the same way.
- **Marker highlight.** A rough-edged salmon marker swipes left to right across a real README heading in about 0.4 s.
- **Command chip.** A small pill in monospace names the skill being shown (`/linkedin-post-writer`), with the pixel Claude mascot, above the card it drives. A status chip ("Checking before posting…", with a spinner) does the same for a running step.
- **Flags.** Red squiggles under two lines and red "Sounds AI-written" tags pop in on the word, then a checklist ticks: two green "Passed", one red.
- **Step rail.** "Topics, Hooks, Best time" with ticks, the current step in the accent colour.

## Type

- Over the speaker: one word at a time in a bold sans, lowercase, about 64 px, low on the chest.
- Over full-frame UI: one word at a time in a condensed italic serif, all caps, about 70 px, in a fixed lane under the card. The switch tells the viewer "this is the screen" against "this is me".
- **Stacked emphasis**, twice: "completely / free" and "comment / Agent". A small italic serif word, then a big bold word that rises from below out of a blur into place, in a warm orange gradient with a glow.

## Motion

- Text arrives with a short blur, not a fade.
- Cards and elements land with a small rise. No bounce.
- A slow push on the talking head through each shot.

## Sound (read from spectrograms, not heard)

- A music bed, compressed hard under the voice.
- **Risers into the big changes:** a rising sweep of about 0.6 s that ends on the cut into a full-frame UI shot (at 1.6 s and 12.4 s).
- **Glints:** short high tones (about 12 to 14 kHz) as UI elements land: the planner times, the counter, the checklist.
- Soft clicks with some reveals. The typing itself is quiet under the voice.
- This differs from the five earlier reels, which put no sound on cuts. Here a sound leads into a big change, but not into every cut.

## What we take, and what we don't

Take: typing with a cursor, the rolling counter with blur, the marker swipe, the command chip, blur-in text, the stacked emphasis (in our type and colours), risers into full-frame graphics, glints on reveals, crisp rebuilt UI drawn from real output.

Leave: made-up people and posts, the orange glow gradient (off palette), a third typeface, and the soft-edged speaker panel (our hard seam is cleaner and we chose it).
